HOW TO PLAY

Learn as you explore. Every answer brings the kingdom closer to being restored.

📜

1. Study

Read the notes, or open the study sheet to see every answer with its explanation.

⚔️

2. Battle

Each battle picks random questions and shuffles the answers, so you can't memorize the order. You get 2 minutes per question. Keys 1-5 pick an answer, Enter submits.

🔥

3. Build combos

Fast answers and winning streaks earn bonus XP. Wrong answers cost a heart and show you why.

👑

4. Face the Fog King

Gain levels to unlock the Final Boss. His questions come from every category at once.
